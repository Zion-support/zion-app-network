Interactive local scenario calculator, not AI inference, observed savings or a guaranteed return. Validate manual assumptions before implementation. Inputs are not uploaded, stored or emailed. Zero-investment ROI is not applicable. Shared accessible layout, label associations and translated invalid-input/no-payback states.
