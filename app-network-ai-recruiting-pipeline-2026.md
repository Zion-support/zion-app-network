# App Network — New Concept App: AI Recruiting Pipeline Agent

**Cluster:** Sales & Lead Generation · **Batch:** 127 (2026-11-14) · **Status:** Published (5-language HTML) + repo in GitHub  
**Owner:** Zion Tech Group · **Lead:** Apollo specialist + TalentOps

## 1. Purpose

A free, always-online **AI Recruiting Pipeline Agent** hub: the intelligence layer for end-to-end hiring automation.
It automates the recruiting pipeline — sourcing, candidate screening, interview scheduling, engagement, and
ATS/CRM write-back — so the company's FE/technician hiring and service staffing happen faster and with less
manual effort. Builds on Batch 125 (AI SDR) but turns it inward: hiring the people who deliver the service.

## 2. Why now (market signal)

- AI recruiting agents do the full pipeline: sourcing (sourcing-led hiring), screening (score + prioritise), outreach,
  interview scheduling, video interviews, and ATS write-back — keeping the recruiter in the approval loop for
  strategy decisions (Eightfold, Paradox, hireEZ, Workable, The Cognitive, Humanly, Manatal, Lever, Greenhouse).
- Multi-agent orchestration: sourcing agent → voice-screening agent → scheduling agent → concierge agent, all
  coordinated. Benchmarks popular in 2026.
- The company's service business needs reliable FE/technician recruitment — this is the pipeline that fills the
  service-delivery roles.

## 3. Core features

1. **Sourcing Agent** — searches LinkedIn/job boards/professional networks for FE/technician candidates; rank by fit.
2. **Screening Agent** — conversational AI screening (chat/voice) that scores candidates against role criteria.
3. **Interview Scheduling Agent** — resolves the three-sided scheduling problem (candidate × hiring manager ×
   interviewer) via calendar integration.
4. **Candidate Engagement Agent** — 24/7 conversational engagement: instant answers, status updates, interview
   reminders, offered feedback.
5. **ATS/CRM Write-Back** — candidate records + interview outcomes written to the ATS/CRM (HubSpot), with audit trail.
6. **Pipeline & Time-to-Hire Analytics** — dashboards for sourcing sources, time-to-fill, quality-of-hire.

## 4. Pricing & markup (Zion)

- **AI Recruiting Pipeline Agent:** free, always online (sourcing + screening pipeline overview).
- **ATS/CRM write-back, video interview scheduling, analytics:** priced per engagement, **minimum 40% markup over
  all-in cost for Zion**.
- No order or write-back begins without client written confirmation + PO + Zion release.
