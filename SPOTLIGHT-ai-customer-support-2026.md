## Apps in this cluster (6)

2. 🧭 **Ticket Triage & Routing** — classifies incoming requests by intent/sentiment and routes to the right queue, team, or escalation path.
3. ✅ **Resolution Agent** — takes actions: query records, update tickets, trigger workflows, and hand off complex cases with full context.
4. 🌐 **Multilingual Support (5 langs)** — EN/PT-BR/ES/FR/DE support, consistent with the 5-language network.
5. 🙋 **Human Handoff** — escalates complex cases to human agents with full conversation context preserved.
6. 📈 **Support Analytics** — resolution rate, deflection rate, avg. handle time, CSAT by topic and language.
