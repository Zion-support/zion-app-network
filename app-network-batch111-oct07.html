Plan a controlled pilot

  1. Choose one workflow and measure the current baseline.
  2. Use synthetic or approved non-sensitive data; document assumptions and human review.
  3. Set a success metric and stop rule. Obtain separate approval before implementation.

Do not automate veterinary, food-safety, irrigation or financial decisions from these concepts. Qualified specialists and applicable controls remain required.
