Put the guide into practice

  1. Choose one bounded question

    Select an onboarding checklist, a coverage review or one metered facility process. Name the reviewer and the decision to be supported; avoid mixing personal assessment with resource planning.

  2. Check inputs and assumptions

    Document permissions, measurement units, dates, missing data and relevant operating conditions. Use fictional or minimized inputs for an initial walkthrough, then define how real evidence would be authorized.

  3. Review before any action

    Compare the scenario with a baseline and list unresolved assumptions. People approve staffing decisions and qualified operators approve physical changes; keep a record of what was actually verified.

Worked example

Hypothetical example: an office energy review

A fictional office team investigates electricity use outside agreed opening hours. It records meter boundaries and dates, checks the equipment schedule with a facilities specialist and plans a controlled comparison. The scenario contains no employee monitoring and makes no automatic equipment change. Any proposed saving remains an estimate until comparable measurements and exceptions are reviewed.
