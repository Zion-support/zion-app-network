## Why it matters

- End-to-end hiring automation: sourcing (sourcing-led) → conversational screening → interview scheduling → candidate
  engagement → ATS/CRM write-back, with recruiter-in-the-loop approvals. Multi-agent: sourcing → voice-screening →
  scheduling → concierge.
- Built for Zion's service business: FE/technician hiring that fills the service-delivery roles.

## Lead-discovery relevance (FE & technicians)

- Sourcing agent finds FE/technician candidates from LinkedIn/job boards/professional networks, ranked by fit;
  screening agent scores against role criteria; scheduling agent resolves the three-sided scheduling problem;
  ATS/CRM write-back with audit trail.

## Price/markup

- Hub free, always online (sourcing + screening pipeline overview).
- ATS/CRM write-back, video interview scheduling, analytics: min 40% markup over all-in cost for Zion; no order without client confirmation + PO.
