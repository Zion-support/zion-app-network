🔁 How the Suite Works Together

When a customer contacts you, Ticket Triage AI routes the ticket. Support Copilot AI drafts the response while the Knowledge Base Gen suggests relevant articles. If the conversation shows signs of frustration, the CS Escalation Radar flags it for human review. The Feedback Loop AI captures the outcome and transforms it into a knowledge article or product improvement idea.
