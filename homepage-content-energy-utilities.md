# Energy & Utilities — homepage advertising pack

Complementary layer for energy buyers and utilities: grid load, renewable forecasting,
carbon accounting, energy audit and optimisation.

## The model

**Field Engineering + IT ops + Remote Experts** — for energy and utilities.

- Grid and plant engineering maintained on-site by qualified experts, supported by AI-assisted dispatch, triage, scheduling and SLA tracking.
- Remote experts and AI operations cover continuous monitoring, triage and knowledge work.
- Energy-specific apps cover demand forecasting, renewable generation, carbon accounting and optimisation.

## Apps

- Field Service Dispatch AI — right tech, right job, right time
- FE Ticket Triage — AI triage of field-engineer tickets
- Technician Scheduler AI — routes, SLAs, skills matching
- Onsite Support Copilot — on-site AI copilot for technicians
- Service SLA Tracker — SLA monitoring and breach prediction
- Field Workforce Analytics — utilization, cost, performance
- Smart Grid Load Balancer — balance grid load across sources and tariffs
- Renewable Energy Forecaster — forecast solar/wind generation
- Carbon Footprint Tracker — track Scope 1–3 emissions
- Energy Audit Copilot — diagnose efficiency gaps and propose retrofits
- Energy Optimizer AI — optimise consumption, storage and demand response
