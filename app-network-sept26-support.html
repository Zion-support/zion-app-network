How to evaluate a resource

  1. Define one workflow and a baseline: response time, first-contact resolution, escalation or knowledge freshness.
  2. Open the resource and its repository; identify what is actually implemented versus a concept or demo.
  3. Test with synthetic or explicitly approved non-sensitive examples and require a human to approve outbound communications.
  4. Record evidence, owner, acceptance and stop rule. Obtain formal client approval and a purchase order before paid field work or procurement.
