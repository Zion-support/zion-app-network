# Batch 132 — AI Data Analytics & BI Automation

**Cluster:** Sales & Lead Generation · **Batch:** 132 (2026-11-14) · **Status:** Published (5-language HTML) + repo in GitHub  
**Owner:** Zion Tech Group · **Lead:** Commercial + Operations

## 1. Purpose

A free, always-online **AI Data Analytics & BI Automation** hub: the intelligence layer for agentic analytics and
augmented analytics. It answers business questions in plain language, detects anomalies before they trigger SLA
breaches, generates operational briefings, and "pushes" insights to your team — no SQL required. Builds on the
dashboard and data content already in the network and the discovery workflow.

## 2. Why now (market signal)

- The biggest shift in 2026 is from pull to push analytics: agents monitor data 24/7 and "push" insights when
  something significant happens (Tableau Pulse, ThoughtSpot, Power BI Copilot, basedapp, Databricks Genie, Snowflake
  CoWork, Looker, Sigma, Omni).
- Agentic analytics: agents plan several queries to answer a "why" question, run on a schedule or trigger, and can
  act — post to Slack, build dashboards, call other tools (ThoughtSpot Spotter, Databricks Genie, Snowflake CoWork,
  Omni, basedapp, Hex, Sigma, Looker, Tableau, Power BI).
- Operations value: anomaly detection flags throughput drops, SLA risks, and quality issues before daily standups;
  predictive analytics forecasts demand, capacity, and maintenance (Basedapp, ThoughtSpot, Power BI Copilot, Tableau).
- The company's operation and commercial teams need a cloud-native data analyst — this is that analyst, pre-wired
  for Zion's data stack.

## 3. Core features

1. **Natural-Language Q&A Agent** — ask questions in plain English; the agent generates SQL, charts, and answers.
2. **Push Insights & Alerts** — proactive anomaly detection and scheduled digests emailed/Slacked to the team (no
   dashboard login needed).
3. **Operational Dashboards** — real-time process dashboards (fulfillment velocity, inventory, SLA compliance,
   error rates) with anomaly highlighting.
4. **Agentic Forecasting** — predicts demand, capacity, and maintenance from historical patterns.
5. **Cross-Tool Actions** — post to Slack, create tickets, escalate incidents, and alert the right team.
6. **Semantic Layer & Governance** — business glossaries, metric definitions, row-level security, and audit trails.

## 4. Pricing & markup (Zion)

- **AI Data Analytics & BI Automation:** free, always online (natural-language Q&A + anomaly detection overview).
- **Dashboards, push insights, automation, forecasting, integration:** priced per engagement, **minimum 40% markup
  over all-in cost for Zion**.
- No order begins without client written confirmation + PO.

## 5. Discovery & pitch

- Free Discovery questionnaire → AI Analytics hub → your data analyst: answer any business question in plain English,
  get anomaly alerts before they break, and get weekly operational briefings — no SQL, no BI license needed.
- Pitch: "Ask your data anything, get it answered — and know before things break."
