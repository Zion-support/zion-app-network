Make the next equipment decision with evidence

A damaged device, a short-term capacity gap and end-of-life hardware need different decisions. Start with the operational problem, then compare repair, rent, buy and responsible recycling with the same review criteria.

01

Repair and extend service life

Record the fault, operational impact, part compatibility, support entitlement, replacement window and test evidence. Confirm who authorizes change before replacing a component.

02

Rent for a defined window

Specify duration, configuration, delivery and return condition, logistics, insurance, access requirements and support. Confirm availability and total cost before offering a rental.

03

Buy for lasting capacity

Compare compatible specifications, warranty, service level, origin, lead time, freight, import duties, taxes and future maintenance. Ask for a written, time-limited, all-in quotation.

04

Recycle or reuse responsibly

Document ownership and authorization, asset identification in private records, secure data erasure or destruction, chain of custody, reuse value and a recycling/disposal certificate.

One shared evidence checklist

  • Define the business outcome, service window, responsible owner and required approvals.
  • Record technical fit, SLA needs, total landed cost and taxes, exclusions, and fallback options.
  • Use only the city for initial external supplier sourcing. Do not forward customer identity, precise site addresses, serial numbers or confidential email history.
  • Compare options on equal terms; preserve written approval and purchase-order gates before any payment, purchase or dispatch.

Discovery → choose an option → collect supplier evidence → review a bounded plan.
