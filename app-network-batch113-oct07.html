Insurance workflows: explore, document, validate.

Batch 113 connects six documented concepts for insurers, brokers and claims teams. Start with one process, inspect the source and define a reversible test.

Documentation, not deployed AI apps: all six repositories currently contain a README only. Their intended capabilities below are not implemented or certified. Do not upload real claims or make coverage, fraud or underwriting decisions from these concepts.

Six concepts to explore

  • Claims Triage AI

    Concept: intake classification and adjuster routing.

  • Fraud Signal Detector

    Concept: explainable signals for qualified investigation, not fraud verdicts.

  • Underwriting Copilot

    Concept: submission summaries and reviewer preparation.

  • Policy Doc Compare

    Concept: wording comparison for qualified coverage review.

  • Subrogation Finder

    Concept: recovery opportunities requiring legal validation.

  • Quote-Bind Accelerator

    Concept: funnel analysis and draft broker responses.

Before a pilot

  1. Name an owner and record the current baseline.
  2. Use synthetic examples; define access permissions and required evidence.
  3. Assign a qualified human reviewer and a written stop rule.
  4. Measure errors and review time before considering a production integration.
