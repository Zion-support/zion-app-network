Operations Evidence Checklist 2026

Use this checklist before calling a field task, incident action or maintenance window complete. It is an editorial operating aid, not proof that an app, supplier or service has performed the work.

Before work

  • Named owner and customer/site contact.
  • Written scope, location, window and success criteria.
  • Required approvals, access, parts and rollback conditions.
  • Baseline or pre-change state captured.

During work

  • Actual start/stop timestamps and material actions.
  • Exceptions, blockers and changes separated from original scope.
  • Evidence captured without exposing credentials or confidential data.
  • Human approval retained for irreversible actions.

Before closure

  • Post-change state checked against success criteria.
  • Customer acceptance distinguished from technician self-report.
  • Open risks, follow-ups and owner recorded.
  • Do not infer ROI, root cause or SLA success without source evidence.
