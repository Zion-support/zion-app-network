## Apps in this cluster (6)

1. 🔍 **Research Agent** — monitors target accounts for buying signals, conducts account research across CRM + external sources, surfaces buyer-committee insights.
2. 📦 **Enrichment Agent** — adds verified contact + firmographics + intent signals from Apollo, ZoomInfo, Surfe, Seamless.
3. ✍️ **Writer Agent (personalised)** — drafts outreach in each rep's voice, personalised by account, multi-channel (email + LinkedIn).
4. 🔄 **Sequencing Agent (multi-touch)** — multi-touch sequences (Day 1 email, Day 3 LinkedIn, Day 5 case study), adaptive on engagement, optimised send timing.
5. 📥 **Reply Handling Agent** — classifies replies, drafts follow-ups, routes qualified replies to the rep, books meetings.
6. 📋 **CRM Write-Back + Deliverability** — logs activity to the CRM (HubSpot), monitors send volume/domain health, rotates inboxes.
