Qualification checklist before you depend on a resource

  1. Define the operational problem and establish a measurable baseline, human owner and stop condition.
  2. Inspect the live interface and source, distinguishing a documented idea, prototype and tested implementation.
  3. Test only with synthetic or expressly approved non-sensitive data. Do not disclose customer names, site addresses, serial numbers or credentials to public tools or in preliminary supplier sourcing.
  4. Confirm location-level coverage, technician skills, service windows, taxes, travel and all-in supplier price. Reserve or dispatch only after written client confirmation, PO and separate Zion release.
