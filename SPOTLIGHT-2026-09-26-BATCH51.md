## How they interlink
1. **Build & move** data with [Zion Data Pipeline Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-data-pipeline-hub/) and [ETL Copilot AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/etl-copilot-ai/).
2. **Catalog** everything with [Data Catalog AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/data-catalog-ai/) (lineage feeds every other app).
3. **Trust** it with [Data Quality Guard](https://ziontechgroup.com/data-quality-guard/) and [Metric Anomaly Alerter](https://ziontechgroup.com/metric-anomaly-alerter/).
4. **Serve** real-time insight via [Stream Analytics Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/stream-analytics-hub/) and [Dashboard Insight Narrator](https://ziontechgroup.com/dashboard-insight-narrator/).
5. **Optimize** spend and performance with [Warehouse Optimizer AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/warehouse-optimizer-ai/).
