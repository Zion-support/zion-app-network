# App Network — New Concept App: AI Marketing Campaign Agent

**Cluster:** Sales & Lead Generation · **Batch:** 129 (2026-11-14) · **Status:** Published (5-language HTML) + repo in GitHub  
**Owner:** Zion Tech Group · **Lead:** ContentPublisher + Partnerships

## 1. Purpose

A free, always-online **AI Marketing Campaign Agent** hub: the intelligence layer for marketing campaign execution.
It automates content creation, distribution, and optimisation across blog, social media, email, and ads — cutting
content time 60-80% and software costs 50-70%. Builds on the 5-language network (Batch 124), SEO/GEO (Batch 120),
and the multi-agent engine (Batch 125).

## 2. Why now (market signal)

- AI agents cut content time 60-80% and software costs 50-70% — content brief + draft 4-6h → 30-60min, 20 ad-copy
  variants → under 10min (Taskade, McKinsey).
- Multi-agent teams: campaign lead agent plans, specialist agents own blog, social, email, ads, and a human approves
  the brand-critical pieces.
- Content is the discovery engine: the homepage advertising (Batch 114) and GEO topic clusters (Batch 120) depend on
  a pipeline that produces, translates, and distributes content at scale.

## 3. Core features

1. **Campaign Lead Agent** — takes a goal (product launch, lead gen, brand awareness) and plans the campaign across
   channels with a human approval gate.
2. **Content Generation Agent** — drafts blog posts, landing pages, social posts, and email copy in Zion's brand voice
   across the 5-language network.
3. **Repurposing Agent** — turns one asset into 8+ channel formats (blog → social post, email, LinkedIn, ad variants).
4. **SEO & Metadata Agent** — keyword research, briefs, optimisation for Google and AI search (GEO), metadata suggestions.
5. **Social Media Agent** — planning, copy generation, scheduling across LinkedIn, X, Instagram, with performance
   monitoring.
6. **Competitive Monitoring Agent** — tracks rival launches, pricing, content; flags patterns in weekly brief.

## 4. Pricing & markup (Zion)

- **AI Marketing Campaign Agent:** free, always online (campaign lead + content generation overview).
- **Multi-channel publishing, scheduling, analytics:** priced per engagement, **minimum 40% markup over all-in cost
  for Zion**.
- No order begins without client written confirmation + PO.
