# Spotlight — Batch 53: Contract Lifecycle & Legal Ops (Sep 26, 2026)

Eight interlinked apps to discover, review, negotiate and manage contracts end-to-end.

| App | What it does | Live |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Tender Scout** | Finds relevant tenders/RFPs and pre-screens requirements against your capabilities. | https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-tender-scout/ |
| **AI Contract Reviewer** | AI review of contracts: risks, deviations and missing clauses with citations. | https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-contract-reviewer/ |
| **Contract Clause Reviewer** | Clause-by-clause analysis against your playbook and fallback standards. | https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-reviewer/ |
| **AI Contract Clause Screener** | Fast screening of third-party paper against your standards before deep review. | https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-contract-clause-screener/ |
| **Contract Redline Copilot** | Suggests redlines and fallback positions during negotiation rounds. | https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-redline-copilot/ |
| **Contract Clause Sentry** | Continuous monitoring for risky clauses in every new draft that lands. | https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-sentry/ |
| **Contract Obligation Tracker** | Tracks obligations, milestones and deliverables post-signature so nothing slips. | https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-obligation-tracker/ |
| **Contract Renewal Radar** | Renewal dates, auto-renew traps and notice-window alerts across your portfolio. | https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-renewal-radar/ |

## How they interlink
1. **Discover** opportunities with [AI Tender Scout](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-tender-scout/).
2. **Review** incoming paper with [AI Contract Clause Screener](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-contract-clause-screener/), [AI Contract Reviewer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-contract-reviewer/) and [Contract Clause Reviewer](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-reviewer/).
3. **Negotiate** with [Contract Redline Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-redline-copilot/) while [Contract Clause Sentry](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-sentry/) guards every new draft.
4. **Manage** the signed portfolio with [Contract Obligation Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-obligation-tracker/) and [Contract Renewal Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-renewal-radar/).
