## Source and publication boundaries

Production uses `public/` plus deployment-time renderers. The old English raw workbench source is not the final production artifact. Preserve `scripts/render-supplier-evidence.cjs` and its original calculation core; guidance is additive in `scripts/render-supplier-guidance.cjs`, invoked by `scripts/render-supplier-release.cjs`.

Guidance marker: `supplier-guidance-2026-10-11-v1`. Workbench marker remains `supplier-evidence-2026-10-09-v2`. [Guidance manifest](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/supplier-guidance-publication.json). Publication status and evidence must be recorded separately; a route in this catalog is not by itself proof of successful deployment.

Minimum 40% markup, separate client taxes, written client confirmation, applicable PO and separate Zion authorization remain mandatory. A checked declaration is not source evidence or purchase/dispatch authorization. No input uploads, persistent storage, email, bookings or orders were added. Fictional example taxes are not recommended rates.

[Prior supplier release evidence](SUPPLIER-EVIDENCE-RELEASE-2026-10-11.md). [Guidance source commit](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-support.github.io/commit/ffb4a174619fcd9345e8db8ddd5dfa5275ac688e). Full-network consolidation and translation remain unfinished.
