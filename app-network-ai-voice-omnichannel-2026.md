# App Network — Batch 131: AI Voice & Omnichannel Contact Center

**Cluster:** Sales & Lead Generation · **Batch:** 131 (2026-11-14) · **Status:** Published (5-language HTML) + repo in GitHub  
**Owner:** Zion Tech Group · **Lead:** Commercial + Client Success

## 1. Purpose

A free, always-online **AI Voice & Omnichannel Contact Center** hub: the intelligence layer for voice and
omnichannel customer engagement. It runs inbound and outbound voice + SMS + chat + WhatsApp + webRTC, answers from
the company's knowledge base, qualifies and books appointments, resolves routine requests, and hot-transfers to live
agents with full conversation context. This is the modern contact-center-as-a-service AI layer (Harmony, Vapi,
Bland AI, Talkdesk, Zendesk, RingCentral, Genesys, Five9, PolyAI, Retell, Voiso).

## 2. Why now (market signal)

- Voice AI agents now run inbound and outbound calls end-to-end across sales, service, and operations with
  sub-400ms latency, SOC 2 Type II compliance, and automatic CRM write-back (Harmony, Bland AI, Vapi, PolyAI,
  Retell, Voiso, Cognigy, Parloa).
- Omnichannel coverage: phone, SMS, chat, WhatsApp, and WebRTC with native connectors (Twilio, Genesys, Amazon
  Connect, SIP trunking) and language mid-conversation switching.
- 2026 platforms: Zendesk, RingCentral RingCX, Genesys Cloud CX, Five9, Talkdesk, PolyAI, Nuance (now Microsoft),
  and emerging AI-native voice startups.
- The company's client-facing service business needs a 24/7 voice + omnichannel agent — this is that agent, pre-wired
  for Zion's support tools.

## 3. Core features

1. **Voice Agent** — answers inbound and runs outbound campaigns; qualifies against criteria on a deterministic flow;
   hot-transfers to a live rep with full call context.
2. **Omnichannel Coverage** — phone, SMS, chat, WhatsApp, and WebRTC with native connectors and mid-conversation
   language switching.
3. **Knowledge-Grounded Answers** — answers from the company's knowledge base, FAQ, and policy documents.
4. **Voice-to-Text + Sentiment** — real-time transcription, call scoring, and sentiment analysis for QA and coaching.
5. **Appointment & Lead Booking** — confirms, books, and routes qualified leads; writes outcomes to the CRM.
6. **Human Handoff** — escalates complex cases to live agents with full conversation context and intent data.

## 4. Pricing & markup (Zion)

- **Voice/SMS/chat/WhatsApp/ribbon integration, outbound campaigns, transcription + QA, CRM integration:** priced per
