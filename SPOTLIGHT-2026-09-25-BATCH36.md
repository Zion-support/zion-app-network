## How the suite interlinks

1. [Zion AI Recruiter](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-recruiter/) and [AI Resume Screener](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-resume-screener/) fill and qualify the top of funnel.
2. [Zion AI Interview Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-interview-copilot/) and [Interview Prep Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/interview-prep-copilot/) run consistent, structured interviews.
3. [Onboarding Journey AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/onboarding-journey-ai/) and [Onboarding Buddy AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/onboarding-buddy-ai/) turn offers into productive hires.
4. [Learning Path Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/learning-path-copilot/) and [Skills Gap Analyzer](https://ziontechgroup.com/skills-gap-analyzer/) grow people along the roadmap.
5. [Attrition Risk Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/attrition-risk-radar/) and [Employee Engagement Pulse](https://ziontechgroup.com/employee-engagement-pulse/) keep the talent you worked hard to hire.
6. [AI HR Talent Platform](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-hr-talent-platform/) ties the whole lifecycle together.
