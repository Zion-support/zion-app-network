Put the guide into practice

  1. Define the decision and baseline

    Record the system, responsible owner, current measurements, units and observation period. State whether the task is a calculation, a planning review or a proposed integration.

  2. Request a repeatable demonstration

    Specify the input sample, expected result and acceptance criteria. Record what the resource actually produces, including errors and assumptions, and compare it with an independently checked example.

  3. Prepare the handoff

    Summarize verified results, unresolved requirements, access needs and rollback arrangements. Obtain the required technical and commercial approvals before any deployment, purchase or field visit.

Worked example

Illustrative example: a capacity review

With a hypothetical baseline of 50 virtual machines and annual growth of 20%, the capacity calculator shows 60 virtual machines in year one. This is a formula result, not observed demand or remaining capacity. Record who owns the baseline and compare the scenario with real measurements before using it in an equipment request. Backup acceptance still requires separate restore-test evidence.

What this guide covers

The linked resource pages currently open in English. A browser calculation or sample status table does not establish live monitoring, a trained model, successful restores or automated control. Record the responsible owner, demonstration results and approval requirements for any proposed implementation.
