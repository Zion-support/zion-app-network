Put the guide into practice

  1. Define the question and owner

    Choose one service or dataset, identify the official source and assign a reviewer. Record what answer is needed and what decision remains outside the exercise.

  2. Build a small evidence sample

    Use synthetic, public or appropriately authorized material. Record the source and date for each item, the expected result and any missing information.

  3. Review before a pilot

    Compare the observed output with the source, document errors and agree on a stop condition. Expand only after the responsible people accept the scope and evidence.

Worked example

Illustrative example: a budget comparison

A fictional team compares two published versions of a parks budget. It records the version dates, currency and matching categories, then asks a reviewer to explain changes. A proposed allocation remains separate from approved funding and actual spending.

What this guide covers

This guide supports planning and human review. Publication of a landing page does not establish working AI or an official service. It does not approve permits or grants, dispatch emergencies, control traffic, certify asset safety or profile individuals.
