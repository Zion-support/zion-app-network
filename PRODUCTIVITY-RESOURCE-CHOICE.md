## English — Choose by the output, not the AI label

Three local browser tools: [Agent Brief](https://ziontechgroup.com/agent-brief/) composes six fields, [AI Email Drafter](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-email-drafter/) uses four templates, and [Meeting Notes Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-meeting-notes-copilot/) classifies by keyword rules. Three manual-review or planning resources: [Admissions Essay Reviewer](https://ziontechgroup.com/admissions-essay-reviewer/), [AI Access Reviewer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-access-reviewer/) and [Incident Response](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-incident-response/). These pages do not process essays automatically, revoke permissions, monitor systems or contain attacks.

1. Choose one small task with fictional or authorized sanitized information.
2. Use a local tool for a draft/recap, or a guide for a rubric/scope.
3. Review facts, categories, owners and deadlines; copy between tools manually.
4. Record a baseline and stop rule before separately authorizing a pilot.

Do not paste secrets or private client records. No purchase, deployment or inbox-delivery guarantee is implied.
