# App Network — New Concept App: Content Intelligence & GEO Agent

**Cluster:** Language / Vertical Hubs · **Batch:** 126 (2026-11-14) · **Status:** Published (5-language HTML) + repo in GitHub  
**Owner:** Zion Tech Group · **Lead:** ContentPublisher + Partnerships

## 1. Purpose

A free, always-online **Content Intelligence & GEO Agent** hub: the intelligence layer for content strategy and AI
search visibility. It monitors the company's own content and external signals to identify keyword/competitor gaps,
content clusters, AI-citation weaknesses, and driving/trigger questions — and packages each finding into a scoped
content brief for the agent marketplace (Batch 117) and implementation vault (Batch 121). This is the "GEO topic
clusters" content layer applied to live content.

## 2. Why now (market signal)

- GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is exploding: 170+ agencies now partner with OtterlyAI to monitor ChatGPT,
  Google AI Overviews, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Perplexity for brand visibility and AI citations.
- Agentic SEO workflow tools (AirOps, SEOwind, Frase, Semrush, Surfer) automate keyword research → briefs →
  drafting → scoring → QA → publishing with human review.
- 5-language support (EN/PT-BR/ES/FR/DE) is Zion's target; content-intelligence + GEO is the engine that keeps
  every language ranking and cited in AI answers, not just indexed.

## 3. Core features

1. **Content-intelligence scanner** — reviews published content against competitors: keyword coverage, topic
   clusters, AI-citation weaknesses, content gaps.
2. **GEO visibility monitor** — tracks brand presence and AI citations across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Claude,
   Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity.
3. **Competitor benchmarking** — share of voice, brand position, sentiment vs named competitors.
4. **Content-gap brief generator** — turns each finding into a scoped, briefed content play with keyword targets,
   driving/trigger questions, FAQ blocks, comparison scorecards.
5. **Localization geotargeting** — adapts briefs to each of the 5 languages (EN/PT-BR/ES/FR/DE).
6. **Discovery handoff** → agent marketplace (Batch 117) + implementation vault (Batch 121) + vertical trust (Batch 122).
