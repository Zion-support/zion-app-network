# App Network — New Concept App: AI Sales Outreach & Multi-Channel Engagement

**Cluster:** Sales & Lead Generation · **Batch:** 130 (2026-11-14) · **Status:** Published (5-language HTML) + repo in GitHub  
**Owner:** Zion Tech Group · **Lead:** Apollo specialist + SalesOps

## 1. Purpose

A free, always-online **AI Sales Outreach & Multi-Channel Engagement** hub: the intelligence layer for outbound
prospecting. It automates the full multi-channel outbound sequence — account research, contact enrichment,
personalised email drafting, multi-touch sequences across email/LinkedIn/phone, reply handling, and CRM write-back —
with the rep in the approval loop. This is the "prospecting agent" concept, built for Zion's commercial team and
the FE/technician customers the company serves.

## 2. Why now (market signal)

- Sales prospecting automation: HubSpot's prospecting agent, multi-channel sequences (email, LinkedIn, phone),
  enriched contacts, personalised outreach, scoring, and CRM logging (HubSpot, Apollo, ZoomInfo, Surfe, Seamless).
- AI writing and personalization: agentic AI drafts outreach in each rep's voice, personalised by account, grounded
  in company signals, adaptive on engagement.
- Deliverability optimisation: AI monitors send volume, reply behaviour, and domain health to protect deliverability.
- The company's FE/technician customers need a sales engine that finds, researches, and contacts leads 24/7.

## 3. Core features

1. **Research Agent** — monitors target accounts for buying signals, conducts account research across CRM + external
   sources, surfaces buyer-committee insights.
2. **Enrichment Agent** — adds verified contact + firmographics + intent signals from Apollo, ZoomInfo, Surfe,
   Seamless.
3. **Writer Agent (personalised)** — drafts outreach in each rep's voice, personalised by account, multi-channel
   (email + LinkedIn).
4. **Sequencing Agent** — multi-touch sequences (Day 1 email, Day 3 LinkedIn, Day 5 case study), adaptive on
   engagement, optimised send timing.
5. **Reply Handling Agent** — classifies replies, drafts follow-ups, routes qualified replies to the rep, books
   meetings.
6. **CRM Write-Back + Deliverability** — logs activity to the CRM (HubSpot), monitors send volume/domain health,
   rotates inboxes.
