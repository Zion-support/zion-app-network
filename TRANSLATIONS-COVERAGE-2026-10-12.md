Supplement to [TRANSLATIONS-STATUS](TRANSLATIONS-STATUS.md). Existing release history remains unchanged. The [production artifact report](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/network-publication-coverage.json) is machine-readable route evidence, not a guarantee that every body is translated or every application works.

## What changed

- Recognize language folders and filename suffixes such as `.pt-br.html`, `.es.html`, `.fr.html`, `.de.html` and supported hyphen variants.
- Group related routes once, retaining each locale's route variants and preferring an existing self-canonical destination. Existing canonical tags and aliases are not rewritten.
- Exclude redirects, incomplete bodies and missing/unsupported declared languages from positive coverage. A filename alone is not translation proof.
- Reconcile the report and owned language-navigation paragraphs AFTER all production renderers. Existing homepage promotions, forms, scripts, delivery recipients and commercial logic are preserved.
- Keep fixture, full-artifact and GET-only live checks in the existing deployment workflow. No new recurring monitor, schedule or permissions.
