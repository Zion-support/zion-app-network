# Spotlight — Batch 60: Healthcare & Life Sciences Ops AI (Sep 27, 2026)

Eight interlinked apps to run hospitals, clinics and pharma operations — one connected healthcare-ops toolchain.

| App | What it does | Live |
|---|---|---|
| **Patient Flow Optimizer** | Hospital patient flow, bed management and discharge planning | https://ziontechgroup.com/patient-flow-optimizer/ |
| **Clinical Doc Copilot** | Clinical documentation — note summarization, coding hints and chart completeness | https://ziontechgroup.com/clinical-doc-copilot/ |
| **Medical Billing Scrubber** | Claims scrubbing, denial prevention and coding validation | https://ziontechgroup.com/medical-billing-scrubber/ |
| **Telehealth Scheduler AI** | Telehealth scheduling, reminders and intake triage | https://ziontechgroup.com/telehealth-scheduler-ai/ |
| **HIPAA Compliance Scanner** | HIPAA readiness scanning, evidence collection and remediation tracking | https://ziontechgroup.com/hipaa-compliance-scanner/ |
| **Pharma Coldchain Monitor** | Cold-chain excursion monitoring and alerts for pharma logistics | https://ziontechgroup.com/pharma-coldchain-monitor/ |
| **Clinical Trial Matcher** | Match patients to clinical trials from eligibility criteria and records | https://ziontechgroup.com/clinical-trial-matcher/ |
| **Care Gap Radar** | Detect care gaps, build outreach lists and track quality measures (HEDIS) | https://ziontechgroup.com/care-gap-radar/ |

## How they interlink
1. **Access** patients with [Telehealth Scheduler AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/telehealth-scheduler-ai/) and close gaps with [Care Gap Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/care-gap-radar/).
2. **Deliver** care efficiently with [Patient Flow Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/patient-flow-optimizer/) and document it with [Clinical Doc Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/clinical-doc-copilot/).
3. **Comply & collect** via [HIPAA Compliance Scanner](https://ziontechgroup.com/hipaa-compliance-scanner/) and [Medical Billing Scrubber](https://ziontechgroup.com/medical-billing-scrubber/).
4. **Research & supply** with [Clinical Trial Matcher](https://ziontechgroup.com/clinical-trial-matcher/) and [Pharma Coldchain Monitor](https://ziontechgroup.com/pharma-coldchain-monitor/).
