## Apps in this cluster (6)

1. 🔍 **Sourcing Agent** — searches LinkedIn, job boards, and professional networks for FE/technician candidates, ranked by fit.
2. 📝 **Screening Agent (conversational)** — chat/voice AI screening that scores candidates against role criteria, with interviewer confirmation.
3. 📅 **Interview Scheduling Agent** — resolves the three-sided scheduling problem (candidate × hiring manager × interviewer) via calendar integration.
4. 🗣️ **Candidate Engagement Agent** — 24/7 conversational engagement: instant answers, status updates, interview reminders, feedback delivery.
5. 📋 **ATS/CRM Write-Back** — writes candidate records and interview outcomes to the ATS/CRM (HubSpot), with audit trail.
6. 📊 **Pipeline & Time-to-Hire Analytics** — dashboards for sourcing sources, time-to-fill, quality-of-hire, and pipeline health.
