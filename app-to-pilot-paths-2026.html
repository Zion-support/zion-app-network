App-to-Pilot Paths 2026

Turn a published Zion resource into a bounded, reviewable pilot.

Start with free Discovery, choose one resource, define evidence, then run a small pilot with a named owner, success metric and stop rule.

1. Discover

Use the free online Discovery to identify the operating problem, baseline and likely resource path.

2. Choose one resource

Select one app, tool or guide. Verify the destination and capability before planning production use.

3. Define evidence

Record owner, expected outcome, baseline, acceptance evidence, risks and review date.

4. Pilot with limits

Run a reversible pilot with human review, bounded access, explicit stop conditions and no hidden commitment.

Privacy-first pilot checklist

  • Use synthetic or explicitly approved non-sensitive examples. Never submit client names, exact addresses, serial numbers or credentials to public tools.
  • Name a human reviewer, capture the baseline and acceptance evidence, and define a rollback or stop rule before testing.
  • This guide does not authorize paid work, hardware purchases, supplier commitments or technician dispatch.
