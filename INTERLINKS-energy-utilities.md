## Why this vertical

Energy buyers and utilities face rising grid volatility, decarbonisation mandates and
cost pressure. The bundle covers demand (optimise), supply (forecast), evidence (track
emissions) and assurance (audit) — a complete decarbonisation and reliability stack.
