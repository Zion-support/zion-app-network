## English — Find a clear path through the Zion app network

Start with the task you need to complete. Follow a practical guide or a local tool, then carry its result into the next step with a named reviewer.

The navigation and featured guides are available in five languages. Source indexes and older destinations may use English or mixed languages; each link identifies that scope. Review a resource’s stated capabilities before using its output.

[Open this guide in English](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-app-network/catalog-navigation.html)

<a id="start-here-en"></a>

### Choose your starting point

Use Discovery to organize the problem, or go straight to a pilot or evidence review.

- [Free online Discovery](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/): Map a process and read the report in the browser. Requested email delivery is separate from confirmed inbox receipt.
- [First measured win guide](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/discovery-first-win-guide.html): Set a baseline, an owner and a small experiment before widening the scope.
- [App selection evidence scorecard](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-app-network/app-selection-evidence-scorecard-2026.html): Compare the evidence behind a resource and identify what still needs review.

<a id="resource-path-en"></a>

### Prepare work and review it

Use the collections to find local draft tools, manual review guides and technical planning resources.

- [Productivity and review collection](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-app-network/CATALOG-PRODUCTIVITY-REVIEW.html#en): Find the brief composer, email templates, meeting-note tool and three manual review or planning guides.
- [SQL, planning and observability collection](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-app-network/CATALOG-DEVOPS-REVIEW.html#en): Compare local technical tools and planning resources using their stated capabilities.
- [Mining and metals guide](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/mining-discovery-guide.html): Explore documented mining concepts and the evidence needed to evaluate them.

Move from scope and evidence to visit handoff and a completion record. Written client confirmation and a PO are required before a supplier commitment or dispatch.

### Check source records and history

The source indexes and historical record remain available. Historical totals and release notes describe their recorded dates, not a current count of working apps.

- [Master app index](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-app-network/blob/main/APPS_INDEX.md): Browse app and repository references; verify each destination before use.
- [Legacy catalog](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-app-network/blob/main/CATALOG.md): Consult the preserved category and collection index.
- [Mining source supplement](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-app-network/blob/main/CATALOG-MINING.md): Inspect the documented concepts and their source links.
- [Communications source supplement](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-app-network/blob/main/CATALOG-COMMUNICATIONS.md): Inspect the communications navigation and validation notes.
- [Navigation history through 30 October 2026](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-app-network/blob/main/docs/catalog-navigation-history-2026-10-30.md): Read the preserved release notes and original destinations in their historical context.

### Three steps through the network

1. **Choose one outcome.** Name the task, owner and preferred language. Use the collection matching that task.
2. **Check what the resource does.** Read the capability description and source evidence. A guide, local calculation and connected service require different checks.
3. **Carry the result forward.** Keep the assumptions and open questions with the result. Ask the responsible person to review the next step before acting.

### Example: prepare a switch replacement

For a fictional maintenance visit, start with the handoff checklist to review scope, access and parts. Use the operations checklist to define the expected connectivity tests, then the completion pack to record results, open issues and customer acceptance separately. For one site or several, document the actual visit sequence and approved scope before any additional commitment.

### Três etapas pela rede

1. **Escolha um resultado.** Identifique a tarefa, o responsável e o idioma. Use a coleção correspondente à tarefa.
2. **Confira a capacidade do recurso.** Leia a descrição e as evidências de origem. Um guia, um cálculo local e um serviço conectado exigem verificações diferentes.
3. **Leve o resultado à próxima etapa.** Mantenha as premissas e perguntas abertas junto do resultado. Peça ao responsável que revise a próxima etapa antes da execução.

Para um ou vários locais, documente a sequência real das visitas e o escopo aprovado antes de qualquer compromisso adicional.

### Trois étapes dans le réseau

1. **Choisissez un résultat.** Identifiez la tâche, son responsable et la langue souhaitée. Utilisez la collection correspondante.
2. **Vérifiez les capacités de la ressource.** Lisez la description et les preuves de source. Un guide, un calcul local et un service connecté demandent des vérifications différentes.
3. **Transmettez le résultat à la suite du parcours.** Conservez les hypothèses et les questions ouvertes avec le résultat. Faites relire la prochaine étape par le responsable avant d’agir.

### Exemple : préparer le remplacement d’un commutateur

Pour une intervention de maintenance fictive, commencez par la liste de préparation afin de revoir le périmètre, les accès et les pièces. Utilisez la liste opérationnelle pour définir les tests de connectivité attendus, puis le dossier de clôture pour distinguer résultats, problèmes ouverts et acceptation du client. Pour un ou plusieurs sites, documentez l’ordre réel des visites et le périmètre approuvé avant tout engagement supplémentaire.
