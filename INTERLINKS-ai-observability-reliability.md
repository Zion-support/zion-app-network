# Zion App Network — AI Observability & Reliability (Batch 11b)

Five apps for AI operations: observability, SLA tracking, incident response, runbooks and agent workflows.

## Why this vertical

AI agents are crossing into production, but operators cannot see what they do at run time. This vertical closes the loop: monitor, benchmark, respond and remediate. It pairs field services (on-site) with remote AI operations.
