Put the guide into practice

  1. Select one property task

    Choose valuation, lease review, screening, an advertisement, maintenance or market research. Define the owner and the decision the trial will inform.

  2. Prepare reviewable inputs

    Use appropriate samples, record their sources and identify missing information. Specify the criteria for checking the output against original documents or an agreed baseline.

  3. Review before acting

    Ask the responsible person to examine errors, assumptions and exceptions. Approve any publication, access change, housing decision or work order separately.

Worked example

Illustrative example: a lease and maintenance review

A property team prepares a synthetic lease and a sample maintenance schedule. It checks each extracted date and obligation against the lease and each proposed maintenance task against an approved work order. Separate reviewers record corrections before any operational action; the exercise does not automate tenant decisions.

What this guide covers

These pages describe evaluation topics, not verified end-to-end property operations. Confirm actual functionality, licensing, access permissions and applicable requirements before using a resource. Valuation, lease interpretation, housing decisions and maintenance approvals remain with responsible people; a screening summary is not a guarantee of fair or compliant outcomes.
