Put the guide into practice

  1. Choose one work area

    Select one question from the six themes. Record the current process, its owner and the result that would make a small trial useful.

  2. Compare related resources

    Open one or two app pages and inspect their stated scope. Prepare suitable sample inputs and ask for evidence of the functions you need.

  3. Document a reviewed next step

    Record findings, assumptions and limits. Agree on the next test or integration only after the responsible reviewer accepts the evidence.

Worked example

Illustrative example: a training facility

A training facility compares a maintenance-planning reference with an energy-review resource. It prepares a sample work-order list and a separate set of meter readings. The facilities lead reviews each result against the existing process before deciding whether a further trial is useful; no savings or automation are assumed.

What this guide covers

This showcase preserves the original collection’s app destinations and adds guidance for evaluating them. A linked description does not establish working automation, measured benefits or service availability. Confirm demonstrated functions, data permissions and review responsibilities at each destination; the guide’s translation does not imply that every linked app is translated.
