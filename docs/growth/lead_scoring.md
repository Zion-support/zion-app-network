# Zion Lead Scoring Framework (Growth & CRO)

## Purpose
Standardize lead qualification during the free AI Discovery so commercial teams act on high-intent leads within the same business day.

## Score Components (0-100)
- Interview: size, industry, challenge clarity (25 pts)
- Timeline: urgent (24h), 2-week window, 30-day window (20 pts)
- Readiness: already using AI tools / wants payment, budget confirmed (25 pts)
- Signal: referral, existing relationship, previous pilot (15 pts)
- Company stage: funded, growing, multi-site (15 pts)

## Thresholds (immediate commercial routing)
- 70-100: highest priority - same-day outreach, discovery deep-dive
- 40-69: qualified - next-business-day email + priority queue
- 0-39: educational - nurture sequence, retest later

## Data Collected (Discovery)
- company, industry, size, challenge description
- selected process/area (e.g., infrastructure, assessment, support)
- timeline, budget confirmation, readiness indicators

## Guardrails
- Do not store or transmit credentials, IPs, or confidential client data
- Default: unsubscribed + no phone number unless lead confirms
- All scoring is internal; not visible to the client

## Tools & Owners
- Discovery form -> commercial@ziontechgroup.com
- Commercial lead owner reviews and assigns
