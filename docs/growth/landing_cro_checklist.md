# Landing Page CRO Checklist — 2026

## Purpose
Conversion rate optimization for B2B SaaS and AI service landing pages.
Based on 2026 industry benchmarks (Magier, Flowout, Skymoon).

## Priority Checklist (ordered by impact)

| Priority | Element | Expected Lift | Time to Results |
|----------|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Reduce form fields to 5 or fewer | 50% - 120% lift | 7-14 days |
| 2 | Improve headline clarity and outcome focus | 27% - 104% | 2-4 weeks |
| 3 | Add specific social proof near primary CTA | 19% - 34% lift | 7-14 days |
| 4 | Remove navigation menu from page | 10% - 15% lift | Immediate |
| 5 | Optimize page speed to under 2.5s on mobile | 7% per second | 1-2 weeks |
| 6 | Implement dynamic headlines by traffic source | 20% - 40% per segment | 2-4 weeks |
| 7 | Add AI chat with qualification routing | 15% - 25% lift | 2-3 weeks setup |

## CRO Pillars
1. **Message match**: Headline and CTA must match the traffic source promise
2. **Single primary CTA**: One dominant action above the fold, repeated at scroll points
3. **Social proof**: Named testimonials with quantified results, near the CTA
4. **Mobile performance**: LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1
5. **Tracking**: Event tracking, CRM-ready hidden fields, consent compliance

## Implementation Steps
1. Audit existing landing pages (traffic source, headline, CTA, form fields, speed)
2. Fix message match first (before anything else)
3. Reduce form fields to minimum qualifying criteria
4. Add quantified social proof adjacent to primary CTA
5. Remove navigation from conversion-focused pages
6. Test one large change at a time; measure qualified leads, not just submissions
