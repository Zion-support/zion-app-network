# Zion Free AI Discovery Questionnaire — Structured Flow

## Purpose
Free, no-call Discovery: 2-minute online questionnaire → instant personalized results emailed
to the lead and to commercial@ziontechgroup.com for optional expert follow-up.

## Flow (technical assistance path)
1. Cidade/UF e setor.
2. Tipo/modelo e quantidade de equipamentos.
3. Sintoma e impacto (parado, erro, desatualizado, segmentação).
4. O que já foi tentado.
5. Data/janela e fuso.
6. Objetivo do chamado.

## Flow (assessment path)
1. Infraestrutura/área avaliada (rede, nuvem, apps, site, arquitetura).
2. Objetivo da avaliação.
3. Impacto/atestado esperado.
4. Prazo e disponibilidade de decisão.
5. Resultado esperado.
6. Dados faltantes para cotar.

## Data captured
- Lead identity (name, email, company)
- Lead contact (phone, WhatsApp)
- Selection (technical assistance OR infrastructure assessment)
- Response to each path question
- Timing (date, window, timezone)
- Channel (web, WhatsApp, email)

## Data not captured
- Credentials
- Internal IPs
- Sensitive technical data

## Fields for commercial follow-up
- Lead ID
- Existing owner
- Facts
- Urgency with timezone
- Missing data for quoting
- Next step

## Completion conditions
- All questions answered
- Selection selected
- Timing (if provided) includes date, window, timezone
- Missing required fields marked A CONFIRMAR

## Conditions to suspend conclusions
- Incomplete questionnaire
- Missing required fields
- Lead requests no follow-up
- Lead is unknown/customer already served
