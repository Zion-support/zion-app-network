# Discovery to Deployment Roadmap (Growth Idea)

## Objective
Convert free AI Discovery into qualified deployments, increasing lead-to-revenue conversion.

## Phase 1 — Free Discovery (current)
- Free online questionnaire, no call required
- Instant emailed results to lead and commercial
- No commitment, no sales call required

## Phase 2 — Free evaluation
- Results reviewed by commercial
- Optional expert follow-up (one email, no cost)
- Assessment based on discovered needs

## Phase 3 — Paid pilot (Zion Plans)
- Discovery identifies priority issue
- Pilot offered at fixed price (e.g., €500–€2,500)
- Clear scope, timeline, engagement model

## Phase 4 — Deployment
- Pilot delivered with measurable KPI
- Deployed to production with documentation
- Onboarding and handover included

## Conversion targets
- Discovery-to-pilot: >50% of qualified leads
- Pilot-to-deployment: >70% of pilots
- Monthly recurring revenue from app network fees

## Constraints
- Discovery stays free; no call required
- No price for services published on this roadmap
- Each phase requires explicit lead acceptance
