# Zion Case Study Template

## Fields
- Client: company, industry, location
- Problem: challenge description (max 150 words)
- Approach: what was delivered (app, service, service + app)
- Outcome: quantified ROI or time saved (numeric)
- Timeline: start -> completion
- Next step: pilot -> scale -> renewal

## Constraints
- Remove all client data before submission
- Do not include credentials, IPs, or confidential site data
- Only include measurable outcomes (cost-to-save, time-to-value, payback)
- Prior Training, Retention, and web administration are excluded from ROI metrics

## Segmentation
- Customer Success & Retention
- E-commerce & Retail
- Growth & CRO
- Operations & Reliability
- Compliance & Finance
- Healthcare & Education
- Field Service & Logistics
- Innovation & Technology
