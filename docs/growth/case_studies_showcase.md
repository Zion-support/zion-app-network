# Case Studies Showcase — Zion App Network

## Purpose
Showcase real results from real clients to build trust and improve conversion.

## Results Framework
- Every case study must include:
  - Client name, company, role, and industry
  - Specific quantified result (not generic praise)
  - Technology or service delivered
  - Timeframe
  - Link to full case study

## Examples (from public website)
- NovaBridge Solutions: Support ticket resolution time reduced 60% in Q1
- DataPulse Analytics: Security audit uncovered critical vulnerabilities; clear remediation plan
- ScaleForge AI: Idea to production pilot in under 3 weeks
- RetailFlow Inc.: Predictive analytics cut planning cycle by half
- ComplianceFirst Legal: AI document processing delivered on time and within scope

## Technical Requirements
- CMS collections for testimonials and case studies
- Placement: hero below section + adjacent to form + footer/security strip
- Named clients with full name, title, company logo
- Compliance badges (SOC 2, GDPR, ISO) for enterprise buyers
- Quantified metric case studies in mid-page section
