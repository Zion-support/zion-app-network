## Choose one process, not a blanket AI promise
1. Identify a logistics decision, accountable owner and baseline.
2. Check whether the linked destination contains an interactive tool, prototype, documented concept or service. A repository link is not functionality evidence.
3. Validate data rights, uncertainty, actual outputs and human review.
4. Test reversibly with a stop rule; measure the result rather than promise savings.
