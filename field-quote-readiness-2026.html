Field Service Quote Readiness

Check scope, all-in supplier cost and approval gates before quoting a field engineer.

A local-only planning worksheet for servers, storage, networking, UPS and tape equipment. It does not dispatch a technician, contact a supplier or generate a binding quote.

Only use synthetic or approved non-confidential data. Nothing is uploaded or sent by this page.

1. Define the service

Identify the country/city, asset family, service type and service window.

2. Complete the cost evidence

Include verified labor, travel, parts, taxes and other all-in costs.

3. Review the commercial floor

Minimum Zion sale price = verified all-in cost × 1.40. This is a 40% markup, not a 40% gross margin.

4. Require written release

Do not promise availability, reserve, purchase or dispatch without written client confirmation, PO number and internal release.

Illustration only. Verify every supplier line item, tax, travel term, insurance, availability and approvals before any client offer.
