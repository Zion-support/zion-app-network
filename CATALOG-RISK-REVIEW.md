## A responsible evaluation

1. Name the reviewer and measure a baseline.
2. Use synthetic or authorized samples; measure false positives and retain appropriate evidence.
3. Define a stop rule. Require compliance approval before using real customer information or customer-impacting decisions.

No automated account blocking, regulatory filing, live payment decision or purchase is authorized by these pages.
