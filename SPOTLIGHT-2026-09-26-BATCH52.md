1. **Assess** posture with [Cybersecurity Risk Assessment](https://ziontechgroup.com/cybersecurity-risk-assessment/), [Managed IT Health Check](https://ziontechgroup.com/managed-it-health-check/) and [AI Readiness Assessment](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-readiness-assessment/).
2. **Evidence** controls continuously with [Compliance Evidence Collector](https://ziontechgroup.com/compliance-evidence-collector/) and prepare with [Audit Readiness Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/audit-readiness-copilot/).

4. **Screen** third parties with [Vendor Risk Screener](https://ziontechgroup.com/vendor-risk-screener/) and [Contract Clause Extractor](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-extractor/).
