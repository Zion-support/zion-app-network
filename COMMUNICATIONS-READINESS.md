# Communications pilot readiness — five-language release

8 October 2026. A complementary review tool for the existing communications apps, not a new production AI integration.

## Verification links
[EN](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/communications-readiness.html) · [PT-BR](https://ziontechgroup.com/pt/apps/communications-readiness.html) · [ES](https://ziontechgroup.com/es/apps/communications-readiness.html) · [FR](https://ziontechgroup.com/fr/apps/communications-readiness.html) · [DE](https://ziontechgroup.com/de/apps/communications-readiness.html).

Seven evidence criteria cover accountable ownership, measured baseline, synthetic examples/data permissions, consent/access/retention, human review and a stop rule, manual rollback, and receipt in both client and Commercial inboxes when email is part of the pilot. Users can review selected criteria and download text notes. This is human-reported evidence, not a safety certification or production approval. No email, storage, payment or deployment is performed by the tool.
