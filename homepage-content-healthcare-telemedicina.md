# Field Services & AI Operations Hub — Healthcare & Telemedicine

Complementary layer: field engineering + remote experts + AI operations for clinics, labs,
imaging centers and providers scaling in Brazil. Complementary to the core field-service
toolchain — not a replacement.

## The model

**Field Engineering + IT ops + Remote Experts**

- Field engineering and infrastructure maintained on-site by qualified experts, supported by AI-assisted dispatch, triage, scheduling and SLA tracking.
- Remote experts and AI operations cover continuous monitoring, triage and knowledge work.
- Telemedicine enablement (scheduling, reminders, clinical communication, telehealth platform) connects the field ops layer to patient-facing care.

## Apps

- Field Service Dispatch AI — right tech, right job, right time
- FE Ticket Triage — AI triage of field-engineer tickets
- Technician Scheduler AI — routes, SLAs, skills matching
- Onsite Support Copilot — on-site AI copilot for technicians
- Service SLA Tracker — SLA monitoring and breach prediction
- Field Workforce Analytics — utilization, cost, performance
- Conexa Saúde — clinical support & workload balancing
- D Doctoralia — appointment scheduling & reminders
- Docway — clinical communication orchestration
- My Brazilian Doctor — telehealth platform enablement
