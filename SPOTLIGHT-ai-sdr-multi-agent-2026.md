## Apps in this cluster (6)

1. 🔍 **Prospector Agent** — finds ICP-matched accounts from your email history + external signals (intent, firmographics).
2. 📦 **Enrichment Agent** — adds verified contact + firmographics + buying signals; writes CRM records.
3. ✍️ **Writer Agent (personalised)** — calibrated to the intent signal + prior company engagement; human review 3–5 min/account.
4. 🔄 **Sequencer Agent (multi-step)** — runs follow-up sequences across email + LinkedIn; adaptive based on reply behavior.
5. 📥 **Inbox + Qualification Agent** — classifies replies, scores intent, routes qualified leads to meeting booking.
6. 📆 **Meeting Booking + CRM Sync** — books confirmed leads via calendar; syncs full conversation context to HubSpot.
