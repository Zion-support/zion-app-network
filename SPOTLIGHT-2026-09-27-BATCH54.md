Eight interlinked apps to build production AI agents on a self-hosted MCP platform, evaluate and assure them, and protect the stack.

## How they interlink
1. **Build** agents with [Zion AI Agent Builder](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-agent-builder/) and [Voice Agent Studio](https://ziontechgroup.com/voice-agent-studio/) on the [Zion MCP Stack](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-mcp-stack/).
2. **Evaluate** quality with [Zion AI Eval Harness](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-eval-harness/) and people/skills with [AI Assessment Engine](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-assessment-engine/).
3. **Assure** operations with [Zion AI Threat Brief](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-threat-brief/), [AI Compliance Auditor](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-compliance-auditor/) and [AI Backup Recovery](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-backup-recovery/).
