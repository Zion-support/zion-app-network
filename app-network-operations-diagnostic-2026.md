**Cluster:** Operations / Diagnostics · **Batch:** 116 (2026-11-14) · **Status:** Published (5-language HTML) + repo in GitHub  
**Owner:** Zion Tech Group · **Lead:** Commercial + SalesOps + Data

## 1. Purpose

A free, always-online diagnostic sprint that maps a client's manual workflows, finds the highest-value bottleneck,
and converts the diagnosis into a scoped implementation plan — with a costed payback per item. The sprint is the
entry point for the whole apps network: free to run, paid to build.

## 2. Why now (market signal)

- Workflow depth + proprietary industry data are the moats acquirers and builders pay for in 2026; vertical agents
  are **40%+ of enterprise AI deployments planned for 2026**.
- Vertical SaaS commands ~56% higher revenue multiples than horizontal peers and NRR above 120%.
- Diagnostic sprints are cheap to demo ($1,500-$3,000) and convert quickly to implementation builds ($5,000-$25,000).
- Zion already operates the affinity layer (interviews, pain mapping, workflow audit) and can reskin this as a
  branded diagnostic sprint over the same apps network.

## 3. Target clients (FE's & technicians — top priority)

- Electricians, plumbers, HVAC, locksmiths, landscapers, general contractors, freight brokers, trades.
- Small/micro local businesses with 1-15 staff, heavy manual admin, paper/Excel processes, missed calls already
  addressed by the Batch 115 voice cluster.

## 4. Diagnostic sprint (the free, always-online core)

1. **Intake** — 10-minute request form (link from homepage) + 3-question pre-screen.
2. **Map** — client walks us 3 of their highest-volume manual workflows (recorded or live).
3. **X-Ray** — we identify the single bottleneck worth automating first: manual handoffs, data re-entry, missed
   follow-ups, compliance gaps, no-show risk.
4. **Cost estimate** — hours/week saved × fully-loaded hourly cost = annual payback. No jargon.
5. **Proposal** — one-page shipped within 48h: scope, owner, 2-week sprint, payback, and the exact next step.
6. **Self-serve demo** — an interactive "pain-to-payback" calculator shown on the discovery page.
