# Zion App Network — Field Services & AI Operations Hub (Healthcare & Telemedicine)

Vertical bundle for clinics, labs, imaging centers and healthcare providers scaling in Brazil.
**Complementary layer** to the core field-service toolchain (not a replacement).

## Why this vertical

Hospitals and clinics in Brazil face a shortage of technical and clinical support staff.
Two force-multipliers work together: field-service AI (dispatch → triage → scheduling → onsite)
and telemedicine enablement (scheduling → reminders → clinical communication → telehealth).
