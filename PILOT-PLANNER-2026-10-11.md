## English
[Open the planner](https://ziontechgroup.com/en/automation-pilot-planner/). Turn one automation opportunity into a 30-day draft with a baseline, an accountable owner, a success metric, human review and a stop rule. The five stages cover scope, controls, a small approved sample, measurement and a build/adjust/stop decision. The tool runs locally: no AI execution, upload, email or implementation approval. Copy the draft or download a text file; clipboard failure has a manual-copy fallback. Language switching clears inputs.
