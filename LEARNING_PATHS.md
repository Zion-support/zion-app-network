## Test before expanding
Name the process owner; measure a baseline; inspect actual functionality and source; use synthetic or explicitly approved data; require human review and a stop rule; record outcomes before expanding. Calculators do not certify compliance or establish contractual SLAs. Landing pages are not proof of live AI inference.
