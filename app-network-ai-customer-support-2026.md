# App Network — New Concept App: AI Customer Support Agent

**Cluster:** Sales & Lead Generation · **Batch:** 128 (2026-11-14) · **Status:** Published (5-language HTML) + repo in GitHub  
**Owner:** Zion Tech Group · **Lead:** ContentPublisher + Client Success

## 1. Purpose

A free, always-online **AI Customer Support Agent** hub: the intelligence layer for helpdesk automation. It resolves
customer requests end-to-end from a knowledge base — answer questions, route tickets, update records, trigger
actions — with a human handoff when judgment is needed. Builds on Batch 120 (GEO topics) for multilingual support and
Batch 125 (AI SDR) for the agent engine.

## 2. Why now (market signal)

- AI customer-support agents resolve routine requests end-to-end (Zendesk AI, Intercom Fin, Freshdesk Freddy AI,
  Salesforce Agentforce, Ada, IBM watsonx Assistant, ServiceNow Virtual Agent, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Cognigy.ai,
  Botpress, Forethought) — grounding in approved knowledge bases and taking actions across CRMs, billing, and
  helpdesks with human handoff.
- 2026 platforms emphasise: knowledge-grounded answers, cross-system coordination, multilingual support, human
  handoff, and governance controls (SOC 2, HIPAA-ready).
- The company's client-facing service business needs a 24/7 support agent — this is that agent, pre-wired for
  Zion's support tools.

## 3. Core features

1. **Knowledge-Grounded Agent** — answers from approved sources: knowledge base, help center, policies, product
   catalog, ticket history.
2. **Ticket Triage & Routing** — classifies incoming requests by intent/sentiment and routes to the right queue.
3. **Resolution Agent** — takes actions: query records, update tickets, trigger workflows, hand off with full
   conversation context.
4. **Multilingual Support** — EN/PT-BR/ES/FR/DE support, consistent with the 5-language network.
5. **Human Handoff** — escalates complex cases to human agents with full conversation context preserved.
6. **Support Analytics** — resolution rate, deflection rate, avg. handle time, CSAT by topic.
