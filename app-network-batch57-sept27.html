Put the guide into practice

  1. Define the review boundary

    Choose one service, time window and intended decision. Record the indicator, authorized evidence, reviewer and unknowns; avoid copying credentials or customer data into a public example.

  2. Rehearse with a controlled scenario

    Walk through detection, communication, escalation and recovery using a tabletop exercise or isolated environment. Check each prerequisite and stop condition without changing production.

  3. Approve, record and revisit

    An authorized person decides whether any real change may proceed. Retain the approved procedure, actual observations and exceptions; review the outcome before expanding the scope.

What this guide covers

This guide does not connect to systems, run remediation, route alerts or start experiments. Test any proposed integration with authorized data in an isolated environment and retain human control.
